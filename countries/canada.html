Canada Immigration & Visas 🇨🇦

Canada offers premier routes to Permanent Residency via Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), and Post-Graduation Work Permits (PGWP).

Key Canada Pathways

Express Entry System (FSWP / CEC / FSTP)

Points-based selection based on age, education, work experience, and language proficiency (IELTS/PTE).

Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)

Provinces like Ontario, BC, and Alberta nominate skilled workers tailored to local labor market demands.

Study Permit & PGWP

Study at Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs) and qualify for up to 3 years of post-graduation work permit.
